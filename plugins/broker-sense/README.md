# Broker Sense

A free AI team for Australian mortgage brokers, from LoadingSense. Your broker expert and specialist business partner, for Australian mortgage brokers. Eight skills: fact-finds, lender research, best-interests file notes, deal plans, contracts, client care, compliance and marketing. Connect it to Broker Sense's live knowledge and every lender line and cost comes with its source and date.

## Use it
Ask for the job in plain words ("write the file note from these notes", "which lenders fit this scenario?") and the matching agent picks it up. Ask "what can my AI team do?" for the list with examples.

## The agents
- **Compliance guide**: Your compliance expert on call: NCCP, best interests duty, MFAA and FBAA codes, privacy, complaints. Sourced answers; ideal for new brokers.
- **Fact-find**: The meeting turned into the client's needs in their own words, what's missing, what to flag, and the documents to ask for.
- **Lender strategy**: Which lenders will take this deal, the rule that decides it, and what to ask the BDM. Every line sourced and dated.
- **Best-interests note**: The best-interests file note in your template, with the cost difference from Broker Sense, what was said checked against what was sighted, and nothing you didn't say.
- **Deal plan**: Complex and commercial deals: the plan for the BDM conversation, what credit will focus on, and the questions to ask.
- **Contracts and research**: Reads contracts of sale, leases, strata and valuation reports and pulls out what matters for the loan.
- **Client care**: Your book working for you. Who to call this week and why, with the client emails and the lender repricing requests drafted.
- **Marketing advisor**: What to do this month and the words to do it: referral partners, past clients, newsletters and posts, within credit advertising rules.

## Connecting Broker Sense
The agents work on their own, from Claude's knowledge, and say when they couldn't check something. Connect the Broker Sense connector (on this plugin's Connectors tab) and they check lender policy, products, rates, schemes and costs against current published sources, with dates. Then set its tool permissions to Allow all, for read and write tools, so it doesn't stop to ask before each lookup or record. Broker Sense is for licensed Australian brokers: you verify your credit representative number once.

**Cost:** this plugin is free. Broker Sense is free in its beta. After that it has a free plan of 2 questions a day, or A$49 a month (A$349 a year) + GST per broker for unlimited questions; brokers who subscribe during the beta pay half that for as long as they stay subscribed. Current plans: https://app.loadingsense.com/subscribe

## Data
Nothing is stored by this plugin: client details stay in your Claude account. Broker Sense receives scenarios described in bands (LVR, income range, loan purpose), never client names, contact details, addresses or exact figures.
