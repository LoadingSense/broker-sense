# Best-interests file note: default template

Use this structure unless the broker's own template is in this folder. Keep names, contact details and account
numbers out of the note: use the client's file reference or initials; the details live in the CRM.

**File reference:** [CRM reference or initials]
**Date and type of contact:** [meeting, call, video, email]
**Loan purpose:** [purchase, refinance, construction, investment, business]

## 1. Objectives and requirements
- What the client wants to achieve, in their words.
- Requirements: amount, term, repayment and rate type, features (offset, redraw), timing, anything to avoid.
- Their priorities, in the order they gave them.

## 2. Financial situation
- Income and employment (type, tenure); assets and liabilities; declared living expenses.
- How it was verified: documents sighted.
- Foreseeable changes the client mentioned (leave, retirement, a new child, a business change).

## 3. Options considered
- Lenders and products considered, and why each was or wasn't suitable.
- Where a cheaper or simpler option wasn't recommended, why not.

## 4. Recommendation and reasons
- Lender and product recommended.
- How it meets the objectives and requirements above, and why it's in the client's best interests.
- Sources for any lender policy point relied on (source and date).
- Preliminary assessment: why the credit isn't unsuitable.

## 5. Conflicts, commissions and disclosure
- Commissions (upfront and trail) disclosed, and when.
- Any conflicts (a related party, panel limits) and how they were managed.
- Credit guide, credit proposal disclosure and privacy consent given (dates).

## 6. Still needed
- Anything the notes don't cover yet.

## Checklist
- [ ] Objectives
- [ ] Requirements
- [ ] Options compared
- [ ] Reasons for the recommendation
- [ ] Conflicts and commissions disclosed
