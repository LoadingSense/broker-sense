# Contract of sale: what to pull out for the loan (default checklist)

Use this unless the broker's own checklist is in this folder. Quote the clause or page for each item.

- Purchase price and deposit: amount, how it's paid, any deposit bond.
- Finance clause: the date, and any conditions on it.
- Settlement date.
- Special conditions: subject to sale, valuation, building and pest.
- Sunset clause (off-the-plan).
- Property type, zoning and size: anything lenders restrict (small units, serviced apartments, rural or mixed use,
  high-density buildings).
- Title: strata, company title, leasehold. Strata: levies, special levies, sinking fund.
- GST (new builds, commercial), vendor finance, rebates or incentives.
- The buyers named, and whether they match the borrowers.
- Anything unusual: flag it for the conveyancer or solicitor. You don't give legal advice.
