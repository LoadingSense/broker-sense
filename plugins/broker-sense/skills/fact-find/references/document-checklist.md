# Document checklist: default

The firm's own checklist replaces this one if it's in the knowledge folder. Ask only for what this client's
situation needs, by applicant. Each lender's own list wins. Lender lists vary, so the broker confirms against the
lender they choose (Broker Sense: lender_policy, topic document_checklist).

## For the broker's file, not the client's list
- The credit guide given and the privacy consent and credit check authority signed, with dates. These are the broker's
  records: never list them as documents for the client to send.

## Every applicant
- Photo ID for verification of identity, by the lender's or aggregator's method.
- Bank statements for everyday and savings accounts: usually the last three months. Living expenses are checked
  against them.
- Statements for every debt: credit cards (the limit, not just the balance), personal and car loans, BNPL, and any
  other home loan. Usually the latest one, or three months.

## By income
- **PAYG full-time or part-time:** the two latest payslips, plus the latest income statement or a year-to-date
  figure. Overtime, allowances or bonuses usually need two years of history (two income statements).
- **Salary packaging** (common for hospital, health and charity employers): payslips that show the packaged amount;
  lenders treat it differently.
- **PAYG casual or on probation:** payslips covering the lender's minimum time in the job, and an employment letter.
- **Changed hours or on leave** (injury, parental leave, reduced shifts): an employer letter confirming the hours,
  the pay and the return date; payslips from before and after; any WorkCover or leave payment statements.
- **Self-employed (full doc):** the business and personal tax returns and notices of assessment for the years the
  lender needs (usually two), named by year (e.g. FY26 and FY25); the ABN and GST registration.
- **Self-employed (alt doc):** BAS for the lender's period (often 12 months), business bank statements, and an
  accountant's letter on the lender's form; the ABN and GST registration and how long they've held them.
- **Same trade before self-employment:** income statements or a letter from the previous employer, to show the time
  in the industry.
- **Rental income:** the lease or a rental statement, or an appraisal for a purchase.
- **Government payments, Family Tax Benefit or child support received:** the Services Australia statement.

## Commitments and family
- **Child support paid:** the Services Australia assessment.
- **HELP debt:** the myGov (ATO) statement.

## Deposit and funds to complete
- Savings statements showing the deposit, and genuine savings over the lender's period if it needs them.
- **A gift:** a signed gift letter or statutory declaration saying it's non-repayable, plus the giver's statement
  showing the funds.
- **Sale proceeds:** the settlement statement or contract of the sale.
- **A first home buyer scheme or concession:** what the scheme needs for eligibility (citizenship or residency,
  prior ownership). Check it in Broker Sense for the scheme.

## By purpose
- **Purchase:** the signed contract of sale when there is one (for pre-approval, the price range and the location).
- **Refinance:** the current home loan statements for the last six months (repayment history), the latest rates
  notice for the property, and the discharge authority when the lender asks for it.
- **Construction:** the fixed-price building contract, plans and specifications, and the builder's insurance.
- **Investment:** the rental income evidence above, and the rates notices for the properties held.
